FAQ

Do we get our own branded app?

Yes.

Every Auxilium deployment includes a custom branded visitor app featuring your institution's name, logo, colors, exhibits, tours, and content. Visitors experience an app that feels uniquely tailored to your museum or zoo.

We are a small institution. Is Auxilium right for us?

Absolutely.

Auxilium was designed specifically to help institutions of all sizes modernize their visitor experience. Whether you're a local historical society, community museum, regional zoo, or larger attraction, our platform can be scaled to meet your needs and budget.

How much content do we need to get started?

Less than you might think.

Most institutions already have exhibit descriptions, photographs, maps, and educational materials that can be incorporated into the platform. We work with you to determine the best way to present your existing content and identify opportunities for enhancement.

Do we need technical staff to manage the platform?

No.

Auxilium is designed to be easy to manage and maintain. Most institutions can update content, add information, and manage tours without dedicated technical personnel. Training and support are included as part of the implementation process.

Can we update content ourselves?

Yes.

Auxilium includes content management tools that allow your team to update exhibits, tours, images, descriptions, and other content without requiring custom development.

How long does implementation take?

Most projects can be launched within a matter of weeks, depending on the size of the institution and the amount of content being incorporated.

The process typically includes discovery, content collection, configuration, review, and launch.

How much does Auxilium cost?

Every institution is unique, so pricing varies depending on the size of the organization, content requirements, and desired features.

Auxilium is designed to be accessible to small and medium-sized institutions while also supporting larger deployments. Contact us to discuss the best solution for your organization.

What makes Auxilium different from other visitor guide solutions?

Auxilium combines interactive maps, audio narration, multimedia storytelling, multilingual support, guided tours, analytics, and optional augmented reality experiences within a single platform.

Rather than providing a single feature, Auxilium delivers a complete visitor engagement experience designed specifically for museums and zoos.
